are not included in this source snapshot. Dependencies are installed separately under their own
licenses; the project's selected license does not relicense them.

[Direct dependency metadata](docs/DEPENDENCY_LICENSES.json) records declared constraints, resolved
versions and available installed-package license metadata. It is not a complete transitive license
audit. Preserve applicable upstream notices when redistributing a binary/dependency bundle.

The included c1-complete, demo-system and holdout-membrane ProjectPacks and source-generated test
fixtures are identified in their manifests as synthetic THOTH fixtures. These THOTH-authored
fixtures are included under the project's MIT license, copyright 2026 THOTH. This does not
grant rights to excluded external material or relicense installed third-party dependencies.

The public Web evidence fixtures are newly generated fictional greenhouse-sensor examples. They
replace the original QA04 results; no original answer, source excerpts, identities or operational
details are distributed. Legacy fixture filenames remain solely for import compatibility.

External-derived 6g-sandbox-hero, sunrise-secondary, l3pilot-regression and opendreamkit-hidden-holdout
packs are omitted pending source/license clarification. public-demo-membrane is also omitted from
this initial preview until its separate attribution wording is confirmed. No upstream PDF or
repository copy is distributed.
