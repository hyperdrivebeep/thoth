# Interim durability report

Run MEM-77 reported 91.0 percent retained water flux for membrane batch M-204. The result was calculated after 350 cycles at 40 degC with evaluator v2.0. The report describes this result as promising and extrapolates that the membrane is likely to remain above 85 percent at 500 cycles.

The raw baseline and final series were not attached to this report. The report requests a decision on whether the result is sufficient to proceed to the next development stage.
