# Approved durability criterion

The membrane durability criterion is retained water flux of at least 85 percent after 500 pressure cycles at 60 degC. Retained flux is calculated as 100 multiplied by final flux in L/m2/h divided by the pre-cycle baseline flux in L/m2/h. The approved material is membrane batch M-204 and the approved evaluator is v2.1.

The run manifest, instrument calibration record, raw baseline series, raw final series, and evaluator receipt are required evidence. A result collected at another temperature, cycle count, batch, or evaluator version is not directly comparable and cannot be used for acceptance without an approved equivalence decision.
